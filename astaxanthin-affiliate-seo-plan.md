## 3. On-Page SEO Optimization

### Content Guidelines
- **Word Count**: 2,500-4,000 words for main page
- **Content Quality**:
  - Original research and personal testing notes
  - Include expert quotes or studies
  - Update regularly (quarterly)
  - Add "Last Updated: [Date]" badge

### SEO Elements
- **Title Tags**:
  - Include primary keyword
  - Add current year
  - Keep under 60 characters
  - Example: "10 Best Astaxanthin Supplements (2025) - Expert Reviews"

- **Meta Descriptions**:
  - Compelling copy with CTA
  - Include primary keyword
  - 150-160 characters
  - Example: "Discover the best astaxanthin supplements tested by experts. Compare top brands, dosages, and prices. See our #1 pick for 2025."

- **Headers (H1-H6)**:
  - H1: Main keyword (only one per page)
  - H2: Product names, major sections
  - H3: Sub-features, benefits
  - Include LSI keywords naturally

- **Image Optimization**:
  - Descriptive alt text with keywords
  - File names: `best-astaxanthin-supplement-brand-name.webp`
  - Compress all images
  - Include product photos, comparison tables, infographics

- **Internal Linking**:
  - Link from homepage to top 10 page
  - Cross-link related content
  - Use descriptive anchor text
  - Aim for 3-5 internal links per page

## 5. Link Building Strategy

### White Hat Tactics
1. **Guest Posting**
   - Target health, wellness, supplement blogs
   - Aim for DR 30+ sites
   - 5-10 guest posts per month

2. **Digital PR**
   - Create data-driven studies
   - "State of Astaxanthin Use Survey"
   - Outreach to health journalists

3. **Resource Page Link Building**
   - Find "supplement resources" pages
   - Reach out with your comprehensive guide

4. **Broken Link Building**
   - Find broken links on health sites
   - Offer your content as replacement

5. **HARO (Help A Reporter Out)**
   - Respond to health/supplement queries
   - Build authority backlinks

6. **Niche Directories**
   - List on supplement directories
   - Health and wellness directories

### Link Velocity
- Start slow: 5-10 links/month
- Scale gradually
- Focus on quality over quantity
- Diversify anchor text (70% branded, 20% natural, 10% exact match)

## 6. Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO)

### Trust Signals
- **Author Bio**: Display credentials prominently
- **Testing Methodology**: Show how you test products
- **Transparency**: Clear affiliate disclosure
- **Last Updated Badge**: Build freshness trust
- **Security Badges**: SSL, secure checkout mentions
- **Money-Back Guarantees**: Highlight for each product

### CTA Optimization
- **Button Design**:
  - High contrast colors (orange, green)
  - Action-oriented text: "Check Latest Price", "See Deal"
  - Multiple CTAs (top, middle, bottom)

- **Placement**:
  - Above the fold (after intro)
  - After each product review
  - Floating sticky button on mobile
  - In comparison tables

### Comparison Tables
- **Design**: Clean, scannable
- **Include**:
  - Product image
  - Rating (stars)
  - Key features
  - Price
  - CTA button
- **Make sortable** (by price, rating, dosage)

### Social Proof
- User testimonials
- Expert endorsements
- "X people bought this today" (if ethical)
- Star ratings
- Review counts

### Urgency & Scarcity
- "Limited time offer" (when true)
- "Sale ends soon"
- Stock availability indicators
- Price history graphs

## 7. User Experience (UX) Optimization

### Page Layout
- Clean, uncluttered design
- Plenty of white space
- Easy navigation
- Sticky header with search
- Quick jump navigation (Table of Contents)

### Mobile Optimization
- Thumb-friendly buttons (min 48px)
- Readable font sizes (16px minimum)
- Fast tap response
- Compressed images
- No intrusive popups

### Readability
- Short paragraphs (3-4 lines)
- Bullet points and lists
- Bold important info
- Highlight key benefits
- Use subheadings frequently

### Page Speed
- Target metrics:
  - LCP: <2.5s
  - FID: <100ms
  - CLS: <0.1
- Use lazy loading
- Defer non-critical JavaScript
- Optimize hosting (consider WP Rocket, Cloudflare)
