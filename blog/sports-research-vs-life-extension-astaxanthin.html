1. Potency & Dosage Comparison

Sports Research: 12mg High Potency

Sports Research delivers 12mg of astaxanthin per softgel, which is 3x the potency of Life Extension. This dosage aligns with clinical studies showing maximum benefits:

  • Skin health: Studies show 6-12mg daily improves elasticity and reduces wrinkles
  • Athletic performance: 12mg daily improved endurance by 5% in research
  • Eye health: 12mg daily reduced computer eye strain by 54%
  • Convenience: One capsule daily provides optimal benefits

Life Extension: 4mg Standard Dose

Life Extension provides 4mg per softgel, a solid entry-level dose that still offers health benefits:

  • General antioxidant protection: 4mg provides significant free radical defense
  • Flexibility: Can take 1-3 capsules to adjust dosage (4-12mg range)
  • Gentle start: Good for first-time users to assess tolerance
  • Clinical support: 4mg doses still show benefits in research
Winner: Sports Research - For most users seeking therapeutic benefits, 12mg is the optimal dose backed by research. However, Life Extension's flexibility is valuable for customizing dosage.
