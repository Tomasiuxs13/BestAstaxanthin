Affiliate Disclosure

Transparency is important to us. Here's how we earn revenue while maintaining our independence.

Last Updated: January 15, 2025

TL;DR: We earn commissions when you purchase products through our affiliate links. This comes at no extra cost to you and helps us provide free, comprehensive content. Our reviews remain honest and unbiased.

Our Commitment to You

Best Astaxanthin is committed to providing honest, transparent, and comprehensive reviews of astaxanthin supplements. We believe in helping you make informed decisions about your health and wellness.

How We Earn Money

Best Astaxanthin participates in various affiliate marketing programs, which means we may earn commissions from qualifying purchases made through links on our website. These programs include, but are not limited to:

  • Amazon Associates Program: We are a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.com and affiliated sites.
  • ShareASale: We participate in affiliate programs through ShareASale, earning commissions on qualifying purchases.
  • Commission Junction (CJ): We work with various brands through CJ's affiliate network.
  • Direct Brand Partnerships: Some supplement brands provide us with affiliate commissions when you purchase directly through their websites using our links.

What This Means for You

No Extra Cost: When you click on an affiliate link and make a purchase, you pay the same price you would if you went directly to the retailer. The commission we earn comes from the retailer, not from you.

Example: If a product costs $29.99 on Amazon, you'll pay exactly $29.99 whether you use our link or find the product yourself. Amazon (or the retailer) pays us a small percentage of that sale.

Our Editorial Independence

Our affiliate relationships do NOT influence our reviews, rankings, or recommendations. Here's our promise to you:

  • Honest Reviews: We test products independently and provide honest assessments, including both pros and cons.
  • Merit-Based Rankings: Products are ranked based on quality, effectiveness, value, and testing results—not on commission rates.
  • Real Testing: We personally test products whenever possible and conduct thorough research on those we cannot test directly.
  • Consumer-First: Your best interests always come first. We only recommend products we genuinely believe provide value.
  • Clear Disclosure: We clearly mark affiliate links and disclose our relationships throughout our content.

Why We Use Affiliate Links

Creating comprehensive, research-based content requires significant time and resources. Testing products, conducting research, maintaining our website, and staying current with new products and studies is our full-time work.

Affiliate commissions allow us to:

  • Provide free, comprehensive content to our readers
  • Maintain and update our website regularly
  • Purchase and test new products
  • Conduct independent research and analysis
  • Keep our content ad-free or with minimal advertising

Products We Recommend

We only recommend products that:

  • Meet our quality standards
  • Provide genuine value to consumers
  • Have positive customer feedback
  • Come from reputable manufacturers
  • Offer transparent ingredient lists and testing

If a product doesn't meet these criteria, we won't recommend it—regardless of potential commission.

Research and Analysis

We base our reviews on comprehensive research, including:

  • Available third-party lab testing results
  • Scientific research and clinical studies
  • Verified customer reviews and feedback
  • Manufacturer certifications and quality standards
  • Industry reports and formulation analysis

Note: We do not conduct physical laboratory testing of products. Our evaluations are research-based. See our Editorial Policy for full methodology details.

FTC Compliance

We comply with the Federal Trade Commission's (FTC) guidelines regarding affiliate marketing and endorsements. As required by the FTC:

  • We clearly and conspicuously disclose our affiliate relationships
  • We do not make false or misleading claims about products
  • Our endorsements reflect our honest opinions and experiences
  • We disclose material connections with brands or manufacturers

Price Accuracy: While we strive to keep pricing information current, prices can change frequently. Always verify the current price on the retailer's website before making a purchase.

Availability: Product availability may vary by location and can change without notice. We are not responsible for out-of-stock items.

Your Trust Matters

Questions?
