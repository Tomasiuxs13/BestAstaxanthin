Frequently Asked Questions

Is 12mg of astaxanthin too much?

No, 12mg is within the safe and effective range studied in clinical trials. Most studies use dosages between 4-12mg daily, with no adverse effects reported at 12mg. This dosage is ideal for those seeking maximum benefits.

Will this make my skin turn orange?

Some users may notice a very subtle orange or pinkish tint to their skin, similar to eating lots of carrots. This is harmless and actually indicates the astaxanthin is being absorbed. Most people don't notice any significant color change.

How long until I see results?

Most users notice benefits after 4-8 weeks of consistent daily use. Skin health improvements may take 6-8 weeks, while athletic performance benefits may be noticeable within 2-3 weeks.

Can I take this with other supplements?

Yes, astaxanthin generally works well with other supplements. It pairs particularly well with omega-3s, vitamin E, and other antioxidants. However, consult your healthcare provider if you're on blood-thinning medications.

Is it safe for long-term use?

Yes, astaxanthin has an excellent safety profile and is safe for long-term daily use. Studies have shown no adverse effects from continuous use for extended periods.
