How to Use

Dosage

Start with 1/4 teaspoon (approximately 5mg) daily. For enhanced benefits, increase to 1/2 teaspoon (10mg) or 3/4 teaspoon (15mg). Use a precise measuring spoon or digital scale.

Mixing Methods

Best mixed into smoothies, protein shakes, yogurt, or juice. Can also be stirred into oatmeal or added to salad dressings. Always consume with healthy fats for absorption.

Results typically appear after 4-8 weeks of consistent use. For best results, commit to at least 12 weeks of daily supplementation.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I measure the powder accurately?

Use a precise 1/4 teaspoon measure for 5mg servings, or better yet, invest in a digital milligram scale for exact dosing. Level off scoops for consistency.

Can I mix this into hot beverages like coffee?

While you can, it's not ideal. High heat may degrade some of the astaxanthin. Stick to room temperature or cold beverages, or wait until your drink cools to lukewarm.

How should I store the powder to maintain freshness?

Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Keep the container tightly sealed. For maximum shelf life, consider refrigeration, especially in hot climates.

Does the powder mix well or does it clump?

It mixes reasonably well but may clump if added directly to cold liquids. Pre-mix with a small amount of oil or blend it into smoothies for best results.

Is 5mg enough or should I take more?

5mg provides basic antioxidant support, but research suggests 8-12mg daily for optimal benefits. The beauty of powder is you can easily take 2-3 servings to reach higher doses.

What's the best way to ensure absorption without a carrier oil?

Always take with meals containing fats, or add the powder to fatty beverages (whole milk, smoothies with nuts/avocado). You can also mix it with a few drops of MCT or olive oil before adding to your drink.

How long will a 100g bag last?

At 5mg daily, approximately 13-14 months. At 10mg daily, about 6-7 months. At 12mg daily, approximately 5-6 months. This makes it incredibly economical.

Can I put this in capsules myself?

Yes, you can purchase empty capsules and fill them yourself. This combines the cost savings of bulk powder with the convenience of capsules, though it's time-consuming.

Does this taste bad?

The powder has a mild, slightly earthy taste. Most people find it tolerable when mixed into flavorful smoothies or juices. It's not pleasant on its own but easily masked.

Is BulkSupplements a reputable brand?

Yes, BulkSupplements is a well-established company known for pure, third-party tested supplements sold in bulk format. They have a good track record for quality and transparency.
