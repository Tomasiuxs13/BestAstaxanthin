How We Research and Review Products

Our comprehensive research and review process ensures you get accurate, reliable information. Here's an overview:

1. Product Research

We research the astaxanthin supplement market, identifying popular products and emerging brands. We look at customer reviews, brand reputation, and product claims.

2. Anonymous Purchase

We purchase products anonymously from retail stores (Amazon, manufacturer websites) using our own funds—never accepting free samples.

3. Label Analysis

We analyze ingredient lists, dosages, additives, allergens, and certifications. We verify that the product contains natural (not synthetic) astaxanthin.
