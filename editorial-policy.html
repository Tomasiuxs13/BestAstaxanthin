Editorial Policy & Review Process

Our commitment to providing accurate, evidence-based, and trustworthy supplement information

Last Updated: January 27, 2025

Our Mission

At Best Astaxanthin, our mission is to provide consumers with accurate, unbiased, and scientifically-backed information about astaxanthin supplements to help them make informed purchasing decisions.

We believe that supplement shoppers deserve honest, transparent reviews based on rigorous testing and evidence-based research—not marketing hype or undisclosed financial relationships.

Editorial Standards

1. Evidence-Based Approach

All health claims and benefits mentioned on our site are supported by:

  • Peer-reviewed research: We reference published clinical studies from reputable journals
  • Scientific consensus: We present claims supported by multiple studies, not single outliers
  • Proper context: We explain study limitations, sample sizes, and real-world applicability
  • Updated information: We regularly review and update content to reflect new research

2. Expert Review

All content is created or reviewed by qualified health and nutrition professionals:

  • Dr. Sarah Mitchell, Ph.D. - Our lead researcher with expertise in nutritional biochemistry and antioxidant research
  • Content is fact-checked against primary research sources
  • Medical information is reviewed for accuracy and appropriate context
  • Health claims are evaluated against FDA and FTC guidelines

3. Objectivity & Independence

We maintain editorial independence through:

  • Unbiased rankings: Product rankings are based on quality, effectiveness, and value—not affiliate commission rates
  • No brand partnerships: We don't accept payment from supplement companies for favorable reviews
  • Transparent affiliate relationships: We clearly disclose when we earn commissions (see our Affiliate Disclosure)
  • Regular audits: We review our recommendations quarterly to ensure they remain our genuine top picks

Product Testing & Evaluation Process

Every product we review undergoes a comprehensive evaluation process:

1

Initial Research

  • Identify products from reputable manufacturers
  • Review company background and manufacturing practices
  • Verify third-party testing and certifications
  • Analyze ingredient sourcing and quality
2

Label & Formulation Analysis

  • Verify astaxanthin source (natural vs. synthetic)
  • Evaluate dosage against clinical research
  • Assess carrier oils and absorption enhancers
  • Check for unnecessary fillers or additives
  • Review allergen and dietary restriction compatibility
3

Quality Verification

  • Confirm GMP (Good Manufacturing Practice) certification
  • Verify third-party testing (when available)
  • Check for Certificates of Analysis
  • Review company's quality control measures
  • Assess product freshness and storage recommendations
4

Value Assessment

  • Calculate cost per milligram of astaxanthin
  • Compare price to similar quality products
  • Evaluate serving size and supply duration
  • Consider shipping costs and availability
  • Review money-back guarantee and return policy
5

Customer Experience Review

  • Analyze verified customer reviews from multiple platforms
  • Evaluate overall satisfaction and rating trends
  • Review common praise and complaints
  • Assess customer service responsiveness
  • Monitor for quality control issues or recalls
6

Expert Evaluation

  • Dr. Mitchell reviews all compiled data
  • Product is rated across multiple criteria
  • Final recommendation is made based on quality, effectiveness, and value
  • Review is written with full context and caveats
