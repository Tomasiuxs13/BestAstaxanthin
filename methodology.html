Our Testing Methodology

Transparency is our priority. Here's exactly how we test, evaluate, and rank astaxanthin supplements.

Our Promise: We purchase all products independently and never accept free samples. Our reviews are based on objective criteria, not affiliate commissions.

Overview

Our testing methodology is designed to be comprehensive, objective, and transparent. We evaluate astaxanthin supplements based on six core criteria:

Potency & Dosage

How much astaxanthin per serving?

Source & Purity

Natural vs synthetic, contaminants

Third-Party Testing

Independent lab verification

Bioavailability

Absorption-enhancing ingredients

Value

Cost per serving and per mg

Customer Feedback

Real user experiences and reviews

Our 8-Step Testing Process

1

Market Research & Product Selection

What we do:

  • Research the astaxanthin supplement market comprehensively
  • Identify top-selling products on major retailers (Amazon, iHerb, etc.)
  • Monitor emerging brands and new product launches
  • Analyze customer review trends and feedback
  • Consider products suggested by our audience

Selection Criteria:

  • Minimum 100+ customer reviews
  • Available from reputable retailers
  • Clearly labeled ingredients and dosage
  • GMP-certified manufacturing facilities
2

Anonymous Product Purchase

Why anonymous? To ensure we receive the same product quality as regular consumers, we purchase all products anonymously using our own funds.

What we do:

  • Purchase products from retail stores (Amazon, manufacturer websites)
  • Never accept free samples or sponsored products
  • Buy products as a regular customer would
  • Document purchase date, price, and retailer

We NEVER: Accept free products in exchange for reviews, work directly with manufacturers for reviews, or allow brands to influence our rankings.

3

Label & Ingredient Analysis

What we examine:

Astaxanthin Source:

  • Natural (Preferred): Derived from Haematococcus pluvialis microalgae
  • Synthetic (Red Flag): Chemically produced, less effective
  • We verify the source and deduct points for synthetic astaxanthin

Dosage:

  • Astaxanthin content per serving (4-12mg is ideal)
  • Serving size and servings per container
  • Consistency with clinical research dosages

Additional Ingredients:

  • Carrier Oils: Coconut oil, olive oil (enhances absorption)
  • Complementary Nutrients: Lutein, zeaxanthin (beneficial)
  • Fillers & Additives: Minimize or avoid unnecessary ingredients
  • Allergens: Soy, gluten, dairy (noted for sensitive individuals)

Capsule Type:

  • Softgel (most common, best for fat-soluble nutrients)
  • Vegetarian/vegan capsule options
  • Gelatin (not suitable for vegetarians)
4

Third-Party Testing Verification

Why third-party testing matters: It ensures products contain what they claim and are free from contaminants.

What we verify:

  • Purity Testing: Free from heavy metals, pesticides, microbial contaminants
  • Potency Verification: Actual astaxanthin content matches label claims
  • Certifications: USP (U.S. Pharmacopeia), NSF International, ConsumerLab, or other independent labs
  • GMP Certification: Good Manufacturing Practices compliance

Third-Party Testing Score:

Full third-party testing + certification: 100 points
GMP certified only: 70 points
Internal testing only: 40 points
No testing information: 0 points
5

Scientific Research Review

What we research:

Clinical Studies:

  • Review published research on astaxanthin efficacy
  • Verify dosage recommendations from clinical trials
  • Assess bioavailability and absorption studies
  • Compare product formulation to effective research dosages

Formula Evaluation:

  • Fat-Soluble Enhancement: Does it include carrier oils?
  • Synergistic Ingredients: Complementary nutrients that enhance effects
  • Optimal Dosing: Aligns with research (4-12mg daily)

Scientific Sources:

  • PubMed and peer-reviewed journals
  • Clinical trial databases
  • University research publications
  • Reputable health organizations
6

Customer Feedback Analysis

What we analyze:

Quantitative Data:

  • Average star rating (minimum 4.0/5.0 preferred)
  • Number of reviews (more = better reliability)
  • Rating distribution (avoid products with many 1-star reviews)
  • Verified purchase percentage

Qualitative Feedback:

  • Effectiveness: Do customers notice benefits?
  • Side Effects: Any adverse reactions reported?
  • Quality Issues: Capsule quality, smell, taste, etc.
  • Customer Service: Brand responsiveness and support

Review Sources:

  • Amazon verified reviews
  • iHerb customer feedback
  • Manufacturer website reviews
  • Reddit and health forums
  • Social media mentions

Red Flags: We watch for fake reviews, incentivized reviews, and suspicious review patterns.

7

Value Analysis

How we calculate value:

Cost Per Serving:

Total bottle price ÷ Number of servings = Cost per serving

Cost Per Milligram:

Cost per serving ÷ Astaxanthin mg per serving = Cost per mg

Value Score Factors:

  • Price competitiveness compared to similar products
  • Quality-to-price ratio (higher quality justifies higher price)
  • Bottle size and supply duration
  • Subscription discounts and bulk pricing
  • Money-back guarantees

Example Calculation:

Product A: $29.99 for 60 softgels, 12mg per softgel

  • Cost per serving: $29.99 ÷ 60 = $0.50
  • Cost per mg: $0.50 ÷ 12mg = $0.042/mg
8

Final Scoring & Ranking

How we rank products:

Weighted Scoring System (100 points total):

Potency & Dosage: 20 points
Source & Purity: 20 points
Third-Party Testing: 20 points
Bioavailability: 15 points
Customer Reviews: 15 points
Value: 10 points
Total Score: 100 points

Final Ranking:

  • Products are ranked by total score (highest to lowest)
  • Scores of 90+: Excellent
  • Scores of 80-89: Very Good
  • Scores of 70-79: Good
  • Scores below 70: Not recommended

Important: Rankings are based ONLY on quality and effectiveness, NOT on affiliate commission rates. A lower-paying affiliate product can rank #1 if it scores highest.

Limitations of Our Testing

We believe in transparency, including about what we CAN'T do:

  • No In-House Lab Testing: We rely on third-party lab reports, not in-house testing
  • Limited Sample Size: We test products ourselves but can't test every batch
  • Indirect Analysis: Some evaluations (like bioavailability) are based on formula analysis, not direct testing
  • Subjective Elements: Customer feedback includes subjective experiences that vary by individual
  • Evolving Information: Scientific understanding of astaxanthin continues to evolve

Despite these limitations, we believe our methodology provides reliable, comprehensive information to help you make informed decisions.
